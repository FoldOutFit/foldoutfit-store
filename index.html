The solution

Turn any stall into a closet.

Fold-Out-Fit hooks onto a standard stall hanger and drops open into stacked shelves, using vertical space you already have. Folded clothes up top, shoes kept separate at the bottom, nothing on the tile.

Universal compatibility

Hooks onto standard stall hangers for quick, secure installation.

Removable insert

Wrinkle-free storage

How it works

Hang. Unfold. Change.

STEP 01

Carry it like a bag

Zipped up, it's a compact carry bag that goes to practice, the gym or your commute.

STEP 02

Hook and drop

Hang it on any stall or locker hook and let the shelves fold down into place.

STEP 03

Change clean

Everything has its own shelf off the floor. When you're done, it folds back up.
