Meet Morgan

The manager-side AI. Morgan turns Quinn's call reviews into ranked coaching priorities and ready-to-run training.

How Morgan and Quinn work together

The two bots share data, so every training goes back to real calls.

STEP 1 · QUINN

Reviews every call

Flags weak closes, missed root issues, pushiness and objection gaps, with call evidence for each.

STEP 2 · MORGAN

Ranks the issues

Compiles the flags across the team, from most common to least, and tracks the trend.

STEP 3 · MORGAN

Builds the training

Recommends and generates videos, curriculums and roleplays for the issues at hand.

STEP 4 · BOTH

Close the loop

Quinn scores the next calls, so Morgan can see whether the training worked.
